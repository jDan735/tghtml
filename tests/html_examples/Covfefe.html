Covfefe (/koʊˈfɛfi/ ⓘ koh-FEF-ee)[2] is a word, widely presumed to be a typographical error, that Donald Trump used in a tweet during his first term as President of the United States. It quickly became an Internet meme.

Six minutes after midnight (EDT) on May 31, 2017, Trump tweeted "Despite the constant negative press covfefe".[3] He deleted the tweet six hours later.

Many media outlets presumed that he had meant to type "coverage".[4][5][6][7] Sean Spicer, then White House press secretary, stated: "I think the president and a small group of people know exactly what he meant."[8]
